Introduction to Gravitational Wave Science

Southeast University · Shing-Tung Yau Center

Co-initiator and instructor of a voluntary, non-credit course. I taught the first phase: twelve 90-minute lectures in English (18 hours), covering linearized gravity, gravitational-wave generation, compact-binary sources and an introduction to detection. The course uses Michele Maggiore’s Gravitational Waves, Vol. 1.

NRGR — The PN-EFT for Compact Binaries

Advanced Lectures on Quantum Field Theory · Southeast University

Four 50-minute lectures on gravitational waves and Wilsonian EFT, scale separation and the method of regions, the radiation zone and matching, and nonlinear tails and multipole renormalization. The course connects self-energy diagrams to radiated flux through the optical theorem.

An EFT approach to GW physics

PCFT / ICTS · University of Science and Technology of China

Three 90-minute lectures introducing NRGR and effective-field-theory methods for gravitational radiation and compact-binary observables.

Conceptual clarity, rigor & intuition

I aim to balance mathematical precision with physical understanding. Derivations should make the assumptions, approximations and meaning of a result clear. Problem-solving sessions and active discussion help students turn formalism into tools for investigation, both in introductory courses and in advanced training.
