Gravitation · Gravitational waves · Field theory

Gabriel Luz Almeida

Postdoctoral researcher in theoretical physics

Shing-Tung Yau Center · Southeast University
Nanjing, China

How can we describe the relativistic motion of compact objects precisely enough to interpret the gravitational waves they emit? I approach this question by connecting classical gravity with quantum-field-theory ideas, using effective field theory, post-Newtonian and post-Minkowskian expansions, scattering amplitudes and multiloop methods. My aim is to understand binary dynamics and gravitational radiation while developing precise predictions for current and future observations.
